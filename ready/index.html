Ninety days. Mind and body. A structure built around you, and the discipline to keep it.

Maktoub | The Readiness Programme is a personalised mind-and-body programme for a period of transition. Structure, guidance, education, professional coordination and accountability, so the change happening around you becomes change you lead.

The principle

The programme provides the support. Your commitment drives the progress.

Progress depends on your participation, consistency, circumstances and appropriate professional guidance. No result is guaranteed. Effort is expected, and it is met with structure.

Why this, why now

Change the system.

Transitions test everything at once.

Sleep slips. Training stops. Food becomes comfort. The mind runs at night. Most people try to fix one thing. This programme works on the system: how you think, move, eat, sleep and recover, and how each one pulls the others.

It is not therapy and it is not a gym plan. It is a disciplined, personal structure, approved by a person, built around your real week, and held in place by daily accountability.

Three phases

90 days

Month 1

Understand & Reset

Assessment, baseline and your first non-negotiables. We find out what is actually running, then reset the foundations.

  • Readiness and lifestyle assessment
  • Movement, nutrition and sleep baseline
  • First daily disciplines

Month 2

Build & Strengthen

Progressive load, physically and mentally. Training becomes routine, food becomes fuel, and the patterns that pulled you back get named and challenged.

  • Progressive movement with your trainer
  • Nutrition habits, not diets
  • Mindset barriers, worked through weekly

Month 3

Integrate & Sustain

The work moves from effort to identity. We keep what works, cut what does not, and build the system you run after day 90.

  • Routines that run without us
  • A plan for hard weeks
  • Final 90-day review
