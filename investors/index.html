Problem

You meet people without their psychological profile, and finding someone compatible wastes months.

National · Saudi Arabia

Introductions run on family reputation, a photo and a few facts. Every meeting involves families, so each wrong match costs months and social standing. Nothing measures how two people communicate, handle conflict or picture family life.

International

Apps sell curated profiles and endless choice. People spend months on dates that were never compatible, because no one assessed them before the first coffee. The apps profit from the search, not the match.

Solution

Relational Intelligence Analysis and Matchmaking.

Members apply through Lumiora, our assessment engine: a short psychological intake and a guided voice response. Lumiora scores the answers and drafts an analysis, an expert reviewer approves it, and only accepted, identity-verified members are matched, with a report that explains why two people fit.

Where we expand

Two tracks. Premium matchmaking for people whose time is too valuable to waste, and licensing to governments already paying to strengthen marriage: against divorce in the Gulf, and against a shrinking population in Asia.

Track 1 · Who pays us directly

People who would hire a matchmaker, because their time is the scarcest thing they have.

Executives, physicians, founders, senior government and finance professionals. They will not swipe for months. They want a vetted shortlist, discretion and someone who has already done the reading. It is a niche, and it pays premium prices.
