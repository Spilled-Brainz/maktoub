WHY I'M BUILDING THIS

My background sits across psychology, human behaviour, analytics, strategy and mental-health technology.

I've always been fascinated by why people connect, why they choose who they choose — and I've always loved bringing people together.

Maktoub is built on a simple idea: technology should not replace the human part. It should help the human part work better.
