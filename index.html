Screening

Our screening voice analysis technology.

The technology adds signal. It does not replace judgement.

The question tells us what to look for. The voice tells us where to look closer.

Question“What are you looking for in marriage?”

A shift“How do you normally deal with conflict?”

Another shift“Tell me about the last time someone was upset with you.”

  1. Question“What are you looking for in marriage?”
  2. A shift“How do you normally deal with conflict?”
  3. Another shift“Tell me about the last time someone was upset with you.”
  • Pitch
  • Pace

  1. Now

    Two minute voice analysis

    A short voice sample is used as an additional structured input alongside your intake. Questions create context. Changes in vocal characteristics around specific subjects show where further understanding, or another question, may be useful.

    • AcousticWhat the voice is doing: pitch behaviour, variation, intensity, pace, resonance.
    • TranscriptionWhat the language is doing: word choice, framing, certainty, contradiction, avoidance.
    • HumanWhat the context means: read alongside your situation, history and what you want.

  2. In practice

    The Maktoub assessment

    The voice read sits inside a real world workflow: your private intake, a structured look at psychology, values and lifestyle, and the judgement of the person making the introduction. The value is not one score. It is the relationship between the signals.

  3. Next · in development

    Lumiora

    The proprietary relationship intelligence technology we are building around compatibility, communication, relationship dynamics, readiness and human connection.

Voice analysis is used as an additional signal layer, not as a diagnosis or standalone verdict.

My background sits across psychology, human behaviour, strategy, analytics, human performance and mental health technology.

Maktoub is built on a simple idea: technology should not replace the human part. It should help the human part work better.
