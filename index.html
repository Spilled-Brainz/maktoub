The Maktoub journey

A better way to begin.

  1. 01

    Are you ready?

    Start with where you are, what you want and whether a relationship fits this stage of your life. There’s room to grow before you take the next step.

  2. 02

    Who are you?

    Through your intake, we explore your values, emotional needs, relationship patterns and the life you want to build.

  3. 03

    Who are you compatible with?

    We look at shared values, communication, expectations and possible friction—not just a list of preferences.

Then we find a comfortable way for you to meet: at one of our events or in a place of your choice.

We turn the process into a safe, respectful experience.

Not ready for matchmaking? You’re welcome and encouraged to evolve with us through our events. Explore new interests, connect with others and make room for the person you’re becoming.

The technology · Lumiora

Better questions.
More informed choices.

The relationship-intelligence system we’re building underneath Maktoub.

The question tells us what to look for. The voice tells us where to look closer.

Lumiora brings together structured interviews, contextual voice signals and human review. A change in pace or a pause is a reason to ask another question—not a verdict about who someone is.

Our long-term aim is to connect these inputs with what happens in real relationships, so we can test what is useful and improve over time. The core architecture is in development; predictive performance is not yet established.
