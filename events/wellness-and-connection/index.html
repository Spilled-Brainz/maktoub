Your hosts

The people holding the space

Somatic Movement Specialist & Advanced Meditation Guide

Raghad Alaish

From within, it radiates.

Bridging somatic science and yogic tradition, Raghad guides the body back to safety. A certified yoga instructor and advanced meditation guide, she designs nervous-system and movement experiences for individuals and organisations, including the Royal Commission for AlUla and MDLBEAST.

Human Behaviour Facilitator & Founder of Maktoub.app

Deena Al Jassasi

Connection starts with yourself.

Drawing on psychology, analytics, human performance and mental health technology, Deena creates spaces for reflection and conversation, helping people make informed decisions, build new relationships and deepen existing ones.

DJ Juel

A DJ whose musical influences extend from her roots in Central Asia to the Gulf. Her appreciation for the multifaceted nature of humanity is reflected in her choice of sounds and tracks.
